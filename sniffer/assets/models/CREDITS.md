Both models are licensed CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and are credited on the
site's About card. They are the meshopt-compressed copies used by itshoss.fun.

| File | Model | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| murphy.glb | Murphy '97 Cruiser - Low Poly model | Daniel Zhabotinsky | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/murphy-97-cruiser-low-poly-model-31f0ac0f03ad42e3bbf236b3ecb2049e |
| urban.glb | 20 urban cars | zairiq-123 | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/20-urban-cars-b8acc30cb32848a4ba2725ca687f0f9f |

`sniffer.glb` is the project's own character: generated with Meshy AI from the Sniffer artwork by the project owner,
then cleaned (gas cloud removed), decimated and rigged with `tools/prep_meshy.py`. `board.glb` is built by
`tools/build_board.py`.
